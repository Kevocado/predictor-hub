# Phase 2b · NFL + CFB site onto `predictor-ui` — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans, task by task, with TDD.
> **Design sub-skills:** `/impeccable typeset`, `clarify`, `adapt` and `layout` on this site. Read `reference/craft-floor.md` before UI edits. Run `impeccable detect` and one batched desktop and mobile round at the end.

**Goal:** the NFL + CFB site (`Kevocado/Sports_Predictor`) becomes the first site on the family system. It gets the shared frame, PL-structured Games page, match cards, honest weekly record, family tokens and fonts, and keeps all of its depth: props, standings, power rankings, track record and the game detail.

**Architecture:**
- Vendor `predictor-ui` into `src/predictor-ui/`, with a drift test.
- Import `fonts.css` first and `tokens.css` last.
- **Alias the old `--color-sp-*` palette onto the family tokens,** so pages not rebuilt in this plan (Data Hub, detail pop-up) adopt the family colours and fonts immediately.
- Rebuild the app shell on `AppFrame`, and the Games page on `RoundNavigator` and `MatchCard`.
- The sport comes from the URL (`?sport=nfl|cfb`), so the frame's switcher links, Back and deep links all work.

**Spec:** [../specs/2026-09-25-phase2-family-design-system.md](../specs/2026-09-25-phase2-family-design-system.md), sections 2, 3 (NFL and CFB rows), 6 and 7 (2b).

## Global constraints

- **Keep every Phase 1 behaviour and its tests:**
  - same-origin API;
  - request timeout;
  - Try again on games failure;
  - picks-failed notice;
  - current-week retry and the week-1 notice.
- **Team identity:**
  - ESPN logos stay; they are the incumbent badge.
  - The hash-derived colours of `teamColor()` are **not** team colours and must not be presented as such. The fallback becomes a neutral `TeamChip`.
  - Probability bars use the family fallback fills.
- **The weekly record counts resolved picks only,** from `/predictions/{season}/{week}` (`status === "resolved"` with its moneyline verdict). NFL/CFB snapshots are pre-kickoff by construction (`tracking/store.py::_require_pre_kickoff`).
- **Spreads are written with a team.** nflverse `spread_line` is positive when the home team is favoured, so the card shows `fmt.spread(home, -spread_line)`.
- **No text under 12 px** on rebuilt surfaces. No Inter.

## Review focus

1. **A week in progress:** finals show Called it / Missed from the week verdicts, exactly one upcoming game reads "Next up", and the others show only their pick.
2. **A final game with no pre-kickoff snapshot** (`status: "untracked"`): "No pick yet", never Missed.
3. **CFB weeks with 50+ games and team names like "Louisiana-Monroe":** no overflow at 390 px. The conference filter is one select.
4. **A `?sport=` value that isn't `nfl` or `cfb`** falls back to NFL. Switching sport keeps the page tab.
5. **Every Phase 1 test still passes.** Where copy moves into the package states, the words stay the same.

### Task 4: Games page on `RoundNavigator` + `MatchCard`

**Files:**
- Create: `src/lib/weekCards.ts`, a pure mapper, with a test.
- Modify: `src/pages/GamesPage.tsx`.

**The mapper:** `toCardModel(game, prediction, weekPrediction, isNext)` returns the `MatchCard` props.
- **Teams:** `left` is away and `right` is home, with ESPN logos as badges and the team string as `code` and `name`.
- **`centre`:** the score `away–home` when final, otherwise the local kickoff time.
- **`when`:** `fmt.kickoff(gameday)` when upcoming, `"<Day date> · Final"` when final.
- **`status`:**
  - final and resolved: `called` or `missed`, from the moneyline verdict;
  - final and untracked: `nopick`;
  - otherwise `next` when `isNext`, and no status for the rest.
- **`pick`:** the favourite team with its probability.
- **`bar`:** away then home.
- **`meta`:** `spread(home, -spread_line)` and `Total N` for NFL. Then weather and rest in words: `72°F`, `8 mph wind`, `Divisional`, and `Rest 6 v 7 days` (away v home), which replaces `(a/h)`.

**`weekTally(weekPredictions)`** returns `{ hits, settled, rebuilt: 0 }`.

**The page:**
- `RoundNavigator` shows the label `Week N`, with its record from `predictionsForWeek` and "Jump to current week".
- Cards are laid out in a responsive grid.
- The CFB conference filter becomes one `<select>`.
- The sort toggle stays.
- The states (`Skeleton`, `ErrorState`, `EmptyState`) keep Phase 1's exact copy.

**Mapper tests** (every Review focus case):
- final and resolved with a hit, final and resolved with a miss, final and untracked, next, upcoming non-next;
- the spread sign;
- the rest wording;
- the CFB shape (no spread, no weather).

**`GamesPage.test.tsx`:**
- the Phase 1 tests are updated only where markup moved;
- new: the record line renders from `predictionsForWeek`, exactly one "Next up", and the conference select filters.

- [ ] **Test, watch it fail, implement, watch it pass.** Commit.
