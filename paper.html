Paper-trading ledger: whether each sport × market × confidence segment has a real edge over the market after fees. No real money, no order placement — stakes in paper units only.

The rule A segment may only be called "has an edge" after about 300 settled paper bets, a paired-bootstrap confidence interval on edge that excludes zero, and a calibration gap that is not widening. Until then it is "cannot be judged yet" — the normal state.
