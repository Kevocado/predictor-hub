Paper Trading

Do our models beat the market once fees are paid? We bet on paper, keep score, and refuse to say yes until the evidence is there.

Does the model beat the market?

Loading the ledger

  1. Log it

    The model finds a price it likes. The bet is written once at the buyable price and never edited.

  2. Settle it

    The game ends and Kalshi's own result decides win or loss, net of fees.

  3. Enough evidence

    The gate needs about 300 settled paper bets from at least 100 different games.

  4. Beats the market

    The profit interval sits entirely above zero and calibration is not drifting.
